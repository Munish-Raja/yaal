Team

Everyone who uses the app logs in with their own username and password. Bills, stock entries and expenses record who created them.

Shop settings

GST

Apparel: 5% up to ₹2,500 per piece, 18% above (GST changes of 22 Sep 2025). Categories marked "Flat" (sarees, unstitched material) always use the fabric rate. Check HSN rates with your CA.

Categories

Name · SKU code · GST rule

Expenses

Everyone in Team is listed automatically, with you shown as "Me". Add names here only for people who share costs but don't use the app. Only expenses paid by a person can be split.

Cloud storage (Google Sheet)

Store your data in a Google Sheet in your Google Drive. Set it up once on a computer (steps in SETUP-GOOGLE-SHEET.md in your GitHub repository), then paste the two values here. Nobody needs to sign in after that.

Every change is saved on this phone first and uploaded within seconds. Without internet, billing keeps working and uploads later. Other phones get updates within about 10 seconds.

Send staff link on WhatsApp

The staff link connects a phone to your sheet in one tap. Share it only with people you trust.

Excel

Download everything as an Excel file for your accountant or as a backup. You can also bring in products from the inventory workbook (its Inventory sheet).

Backup

A backup file holds everything. Handy before big changes, or if you are not using the Google Sheet. Restoring replaces all current data.

Install on your phone

Android: open the app link in Chrome and tap Install app (or ⋮ → Add to Home screen). It opens from its own icon and works without internet. No sign-in needed.

With the Google Sheet connected, every phone shares the same data. Without it, each phone keeps its own.

The app started with a few sample products and bills so you can try it. Remove them before you begin real billing.
